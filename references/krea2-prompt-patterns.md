# Krea 2 prompt patterns

## Reliable order

`A [adjective] [genre] scene set in [place]. [Atmosphere and light.] [Subject action and visual details.] [Mid-ground/background depth.] The composition features [camera], with [subject scale], [balance statement]. The art style is [style], rendered in [palette] with [lighting and texture]. Masterpiece, ultra-detailed, highly textured, 8k.`

Choose details that reinforce one another. For example, golden-hour desert light can support warm red-orange clouds and long crisp shadows; it does not need unrelated neon or blue cosmic lighting.

## Useful language

| Need | Examples |
|---|---|
| Atmospheric light | `volumetric light rays`, `distinct Tyndall effect`, `soft volumetric glow`, `rainbow lens flares` |
| Palette | `cool-toned color palette`, `muted blues and greens`, `fiery red and orange` |
| Depth | `in the foreground`, `in the mid-ground`, `in the background`, `in the distance` |
| Art treatment | `2.5D anime style`, `impasto thick-paint technique`, `lifelike skin textures`, `highly realistic cinematic lighting` |

## Example

Brief: “A girl rides a deer through a forest and plays a flute.”

`A beautifully aesthetic scene set deep within a mixed conifer and spruce forest. It is a bright, clear day, and warm sunlight pierces the dense canopy, creating volumetric light rays and a distinct Tyndall effect. A long-haired girl wearing a flower crown and flowing white dress rides peacefully on a white deer along a winding forest path, her eyes gently closed as she plays a long wooden flute. Small squirrels gather near the path, while the layered trees recede into a soft green distance. The composition features a slightly tilted Dutch-angle medium-wide shot, with the girl and deer taking up nearly half of the foreground frame and the remaining space emphasizing the luminous forest. The art style is beautifully aesthetic, rendered in muted blues and greens with an impasto thick-paint texture, cinematic lighting, deep shadows, and soft volumetric glow. Masterpiece, ultra-detailed, highly textured, 8k.`

This is a pattern, not a requirement to add squirrels, forest species, Dutch angles, or quality labels to every prompt.
